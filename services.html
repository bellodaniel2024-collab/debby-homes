What We Do

End-to-End Real Estate Services

From a single shortlet night to a full portfolio sale, our team manages every stage of the property journey.

01

Property Sales

Full-service sales representation for apartments, duplexes and villas across Lagos Island.

  • Market valuation & pricing strategy
  • Professional photography & listing
  • Negotiation & closing support
02

Property Management

Hands-off ownership — we handle tenants, maintenance and rent collection on your behalf.

  • Tenant sourcing & screening
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Monthly owner reporting
03

Investment Consulting

Data-led guidance on where and what to buy for the strongest long-term returns.

  • Yield & appreciation analysis
  • Portfolio diversification advice
  • Off-plan opportunity screening
04

Shortlet Management

We turn spare apartments into high-performing shortlets with full guest operations.

  • Dynamic pricing & listing optimisation
  • Guest screening & check-in
  • Housekeeping & turnover
05

Property Inspections

Independent, detailed inspection reports before you buy, rent or renew.

  • Structural & finishing review
  • Title & documentation check
  • Written inspection report
06

Relocation Support

Concierge-style support for clients moving to Lagos Island from out of state or abroad.

  • Virtual tours & shortlisting
  • Documentation guidance
  • Settling-in assistance
