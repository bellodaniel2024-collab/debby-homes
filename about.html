Our Story

Built on Lagos Island, for Lagos Island.

Debby Homes was founded in 2012 from a single conviction: that Lagos Island's property market deserved a brokerage as considered as the homes it sells. What began as a small advisory desk in Victoria Island has grown into one of the island's most trusted names in luxury sales, rentals and shortlet management.

We chose depth over expansion — every listing we carry sits inside Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Lekki Phase 1, Banana Island, Oniru, Chevron, Ajah or Ikate, the neighbourhoods our team has walked, valued and lived in for over a decade.
